## What the applicant's records show

According to records supplied by the applicant, this Range Rover was purchased through Copart as lot 42518314. The total documented payment was USD 37,073. Payments included 30,063.163903 USDT sent over TRC20 to the wallet shown above.

The applicant states that the wallet was controlled by Irakli Kacharava. Public blockchain activity shows later movement to an address associated with Binance. The public chain does not identify the final exchange account holder; that normally requires a valid official request to Binance.

The applicant's correspondence and filings also refer to Zena LLC and CAREXPRESS LLC. He states that CAREXPRESS was notified in writing about the dispute and that he met company representatives. He further states that Kacharava paid CAREXPRESS roughly half of the vehicle's value, while CAREXPRESS later sought the full value and the vehicle appeared in MyAuto listing 121662668 in 2026.

These are attributed statements in an unresolved dispute. This publication does not claim that a court has found any named person or company guilty. Current ownership, registration, completed sale, export status and location have not been independently verified.
